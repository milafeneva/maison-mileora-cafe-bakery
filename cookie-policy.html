What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. They let a website remember things such as your language choice, and help us understand which pages our guests find useful.

Cookies we use

NameTypePurposeDuration
mileora_langNecessaryRemembers whether you read the site in Macedonian or English.12 months
mileora_cookie_consentNecessaryStores your cookie choice so we do not ask again.12 months
_analytics_idAnalyticsCounts visits and page views anonymously.14 months
_mkt_refMarketingMeasures which social media post brought you here.3 months

Changing your choice

Select “Cookie settings” in the footer of any page to turn analytics or marketing cookies on or off. You can also delete cookies at any time in your browser settings.

Cookies from other services

Our contact page embeds a map from OpenStreetMap, which may set its own cookies. Social media links open in a new tab and are subject to the policies of those platforms.
