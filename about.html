Our Story

Two Friends and a Very Long List

Maison Miléora was created by Mila Feneva and Teodora Serafimovska — two friends who spent more time arguing about coffee than studying for exams. In 2022 they wrote down everything they felt was missing from cafés in Skopje: pastry made the same morning, coffee that was actually measured, and a room beautiful enough to celebrate something in.

In the summer of 2023 they travelled to Provence for a six-week pastry camp. They arrived expecting recipes and came home with something more useful: technique, discipline and an obsession with butter temperature. Between 5 a.m. starts and long afternoons of lamination they learned that pâtisserie is not inspiration — it is the same movement, repeated correctly, until it becomes beautiful.

They came back with a name. Miléora is Mila and Teodora folded together, the way dough is folded — and Maison, because it was never meant to feel like a shop. In November 2023 the first croissants came out of the oven at 04:30 and the door opened at eight. It has opened at eight every morning since.

The Founders

Mila & Teodora

One runs the kitchen and the look of the house; the other runs the house itself. Between them there is not a decision here that has not been argued over twice.

Teodora Serafimovska

Co-Founder & General Manager

Teodora keeps the house running: suppliers, the team, the reservations book and the rule that a guest never waits longer than two minutes. She handles everything you do not see, which is most of it.

Mila Feneva

Co-Founder & Creative Director

Mila designs what you see and taste: the seasonal pastry collections, the olive-and-pink interior, even the boxes the macarons leave in. She still tests every new recipe herself before it reaches the counter.

Our Values

Six Things We Do Not Compromise On

Quality

French butter, stone-milled flour, single-origin coffee. We buy the best version of every ingredient or we take it off the menu.

Creativity

Four seasonal collections a year, designed in our own kitchen — never copied from a catalogue.

Hospitality

Every guest is greeted within ten seconds of walking in. It sounds small; it is the whole job.

Freshness

Baked the same morning, sold the same day. Nothing is frozen and nothing is carried over to tomorrow.

Sustainability

Compostable packaging, local suppliers where possible, and unsold bread donated to a shelter each evening.

Customer Satisfaction

If something is not right, we remake it — no questions, no receipt required, no exceptions.

Our Journey

From a Notebook to a Bakery

  1. 2022

    The idea is born

    Two friends, one notebook and a list of everything missing from cafés in Skopje.

  2. 2023

    Provence, and then the first location

    Six weeks at a pastry camp in the south of France, and in November the doors of Maison Miléora open on Rue Miléora.

  3. 2024

    A pastry collection of our own

    Elena joins as Head Pastry Chef and the first seasonal collection — four desserts, changed every quarter — arrives on the counter.

  4. 2025

    Catering service introduced

    After a year of requests we begin catering weddings, offices and private celebrations across the city.

  5. 2026

    A new digital experience

    Online reservations, a bilingual website and a loyalty card — the same house, easier to reach.
