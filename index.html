About Maison Miléora

A Taste of Elegance

Maison Miléora is a premium café and artisan bakery built around three simple things: pastry made properly, coffee treated with respect, and a room you do not want to leave.

Everything on our counter is baked the same morning by our own team. Our house espresso is roasted in small batches, our croissants are laminated by hand, and our cakes are finished one at a time — because that is the only way we know how to do it.

The People Behind the Counter

Fourteen people bake, brew and serve here. These four started it all.

Mila Feneva

Co-Founder & Creative Director

Mila shapes everything you can see and taste at Maison Miléora — the seasonal pastry collections, the olive-and-pink interior, the boxes the macarons leave in.

Teodora Serafimovska

Co-Founder & General Manager

Teodora runs the house: suppliers, the team, the reservations book and the standard that a guest should never wait longer than two minutes.

Mila Trajkova

Head Pastry Chef

Lina Stankova

Head Barista
