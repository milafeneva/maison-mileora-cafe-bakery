Our Services

Six ways to enjoy Maison Miléora — in the room, on the move, or at your own table.

Home/Services
01

Dine-in

A calm, elegant room for breakfast, a long coffee or an afternoon pastry.

Sixty seats under soft light, marble tables and a terrace that catches the morning sun. Breakfast is served until noon, and our pastry counter is refilled three times a day.

  • Breakfast served 08:00–12:00
  • Terrace seating in season
  • Free Wi-Fi and quiet corners
Reserve a Table
02

Takeaway

Every pastry and loaf, boxed with care for the road home.

Order at the counter or call ahead — we box pastries in our signature olive-and-gold boxes so they arrive exactly as they left the shelf.

  • Call ahead from 07:30
  • Signature gift boxes
  • Whole cakes with 24h notice
Send an Enquiry
03

Coffee To Go

The same espresso, in a cup made for walking.

A dedicated to-go station means no queue behind the breakfast crowd. Bring your own cup and the second coffee of the day is on us.

  • Separate express counter
  • Compostable cups and lids
  • Loyalty card: 10th coffee free
Send an Enquiry
04

Catering

Coffee and pâtisserie for corporate events, weddings and celebrations.

We bring the bakery to you: a barista, a pastry table and everything needed to serve between 20 and 300 guests. Menus are built with you two weeks in advance.

  • Corporate events and conferences
  • Weddings and birthdays
  • Private celebrations at home
Send an Enquiry
05

Custom Cakes

Celebration cakes designed around your occasion, not a catalogue.

Tell us the occasion, the colours and the number of guests. Elena sketches the cake, you approve it, and we bake it fresh the morning of your event.

  • Sketch and tasting included
  • Order at least 5 days ahead
  • Gluten-free and vegan options
Send an Enquiry
06

Private Events

The whole room, yours for an evening.

After 20:00 the café can be booked privately for up to 60 guests — engagements, book launches, team dinners, with a menu and playlist of your choosing.

  • Up to 60 guests
  • Dedicated host and barista
  • Available Sunday to Thursday
