Meet Our Pastry Chef

Home/ Blog/ Meet Our Pastry Chef

Elena Petrovska has run our pastry kitchen since the month we opened. She arrives at 04:30, six days a week, and has never once sent out a tray she was not happy with. We sat down with her after service.

You started in hotels. What changed here?

“In a hotel you make two hundred identical desserts and never meet the person eating them. Here a guest can watch me finish a tart and tell me it reminded them of their grandmother. That changes how carefully you work.”

The hardest thing you make?

“Mille-feuille. Everything about it is a decision you cannot take back — how far you caramelise the pastry, how firm the cream is, how you cut it so the layers do not slide. It is the dessert I judge other kitchens by.”

“Anyone can follow a recipe once. A pastry chef is someone who can repeat it four hundred times.”

Advice for someone starting out?

“Buy a scale, not a cookbook. And weigh the salt — especially the salt. Most home bakers guess it, and it is the difference between a good caramel and an unforgettable one.”
